Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Rabshop Upholstery and Glass Tinting LTD ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you share with us. This is placeholder wording for the demo site and should be reviewed by the business before going live.

1. What we collect

When you contact us for a quote or booking, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and details about the vehicle, boat or item you need worked on. We only collect what we need to quote and carry out the job.

2. How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, schedule and complete work, and stay in touch about your job. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Sharing

We may share limited information with suppliers where it is necessary to complete your job (for example, ordering specific materials). Otherwise your details stay with us.

4. Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and retain it only for as long as needed for our records and legal obligations.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you. Contact us and we'll help.
